Investigation Guidelines

🛡️
Safe Sandbox Policy

Probes are bound to safe interaction verbs (clicks, inputs, navigation). Destructive operations, cross-origin hops, and dangerous form submits are automatically blocked.

🗺️
State Graph Traversal

The engine builds a directed graph of application states. It distinguishes route navigations from dynamic in-page DOM mutations (dialogs, sort changes, carts).

🔑
Session & Auth Boundaries

When an auth gate is discovered (e.g. login form), credentials found on the landing view are captured and verified across transitions.

⚖️
Skeptic Filter Engine

Browser anomalies (console warnings, benign network 404s) are skeptical-checked. Only reproducible, high-confidence invariant violations are escalated.
